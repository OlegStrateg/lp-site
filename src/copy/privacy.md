# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: July 2026**

## Your files

Every conversion on LayerPorter runs locally, in your browser. When you drop a file, it is processed on your own device and never transmitted to our servers. We do not receive, see, or store the files you convert — there is nothing to delete because nothing was kept.

## Accounts

LayerPorter does not have accounts. You don't sign up, log in, or provide an email address to use any converter on this site.

## Cookies

LayerPorter does not use cookies.

## Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics to understand aggregate page views and anonymous tool events (for example, "a PNG-to-PSD conversion happened"), so we know which tools are used and can fix what's broken. This data is not tied to your identity, does not include the contents or names of your files, and is not used for cross-site tracking or ad targeting.

## Browser extensions {#extensions}

LayerPorter's browser extensions (such as PSD Export and Site to Canva) follow the same principle: processing happens on your device. Specifics that apply only to the extensions:

- **What they can access.** The extensions use the `activeTab` permission — they can read a page only after you click the extension icon on that page, and only that page. They cannot read your browsing history or watch pages in the background.
- **PSD Export** builds the .psd file entirely on your device. Nothing about the page you capture, or the file it produces, is sent anywhere.
- **Site to Canva** needs to hand your selection to the Canva app, so the captured selection is transmitted encrypted to our relay server, stored temporarily, and **deleted automatically within 24 hours**. It is used only to deliver your capture into Canva — never sold, analyzed, or shared. No account is attached to it; delivery is protected by a one-time pairing code that only you have.
- **Usage telemetry.** The extensions keep an anonymous installation identifier and feature-level event counts (for example, "an export succeeded" or "an export failed") on your device. These events never include page contents, page URLs, file contents, or anything that identifies you. If we later enable sending these anonymous counts to us, this policy will be updated first.
- **Uninstalling** removes everything the extension stored on your device. The uninstall page you may see afterwards asks for feedback strictly voluntarily — a few optional reason chips and a text field, described below. Nothing is collected unless you interact with it.

## Feedback you send us

The feedback form (`/feedback/`) and the uninstall page (`/uninstall/`) only collect what you choose to submit: the text you write, any reason you select, and an email address if you choose to give one for a reply. Nothing is collected just by visiting either page.

- **What we do with it.** Submissions are used only to read and act on your report — fixing bugs, weighing feature requests — and, in aggregate, to understand what to improve. Cloudflare, which serves this site, technically sees the request's IP address and timestamp like it does for any page load; we don't separately log or use that.
- **Where it goes.** A submission is delivered to a private Telegram channel the site owner reads, and stored in Cloudflare KV for up to 90 days before automatic deletion.
- **Sub-processors.** Cloudflare (hosting and storage) and Telegram (delivery) are the only third parties involved in handling a submission.
- **Deletion.** Email us at [hello@layerporter.com](mailto:hello@layerporter.com) to have a submission deleted before its 90-day expiry.
- **Extensions don't do this automatically.** The extensions themselves never send feedback on their own — nothing is submitted unless you fill in the form yourself.

## Third parties

We don't sell, share, or transmit your files to any third party, because we never have them to begin with. If a page links to another tool or service — for example, Photopea or Google Slides — that service's own privacy policy governs what happens once you're there.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. We recommend checking back if you rely on the specifics for a compliance requirement of your own.
